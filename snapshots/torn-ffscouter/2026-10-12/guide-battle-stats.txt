How battles resolve
In simple terms, each turn has two core checks:
Can you land the hit? This is where Speed and Dexterity matter.
How much damage gets through? This is where Strength and Defense matter.
The two matchups are separate. You can have strong hit chance but poor damage conversion, or the reverse.
Source and caveat:
Torn Wiki: Battle Stats.
Speed vs Dexterity is published as an approximation, so treat these estimates as planning guidance.
Speed vs Dexterity
This matchup controls hit chance. More Speed improves consistency, but each extra block adds less percentage than earlier gains.
The calculator below converts your matchup into an equivalent “Speed vs 10,000,000 Dexterity” point, then reads the wiki curve.
Example: if your Speed equals your target's Dexterity, the equivalent point is 10,000,000 and the curve sits around 50%.
