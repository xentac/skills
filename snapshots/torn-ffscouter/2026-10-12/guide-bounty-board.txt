Bounty Board
Pay for confirmed hospitalizations of a Torn player or faction, or earn cash by claiming bounties yourself. Both sides in a few simple steps.
The FFScouter Bounty Board lets you pay for confirmed hospitalizations of a Torn player or faction, or earn cash by claiming those bounties yourself. Buyers stay anonymous and every hit is verified from the seller's Torn attack log before it is paid.
Buying: Place Bounties. Selling: Claim Bounties. Payment moves as Xanax (item 206), and seller payouts arrive through the same provider system as the other FFScouter services.

Place a bounty
Open Place Bounties and follow these steps.
Enter the target: a player or a whole faction. The target must already be tracked by FFScouter, so search for the exact name and pick them from the suggestions.
Set the reward per hit and the number of hits. The fee tier improves on larger orders, and the page shows the exact total before you commit.
Create the order, then send the exact Xanax amount to Glasnost [1844049] with the exact bounty:<reference> message shown on the order page. Send it in one go.
Once fully paid, the bounty goes live to sellers. Keep the private status link, it is the only way to track and manage the order.
You are notified when every hit is delivered. Extra or late payments are refunded automatically.
Claim a bounty
Open Claim Bounties and follow these steps.
Log in with your FFScouter account and accept the Bounty Board Data Policy. Your registered API key is used to confirm your hits, as described in the policy.
Pick a target from the board. Faction bounties share one pool of hits across every member.
Hospitalize the target. Only the finishing Hospitalized hit counts, assists do not.
Press Claim Completed Hit right after the attack. FFScouter verifies the hit using the API, then credits it.
Get paid. Credited hits are batched and sent by FFScouter providers with a BB- reference. Read the API Keys guide for key safety, and keep the key active until the payout arrives.
Rules to know
Bounties are anonymous. Buyers are never identified to targets or sellers, and sellers are only shown to the buyer through the private status link.
You cannot claim a bounty you placed or are the target of.
A bounty only counts attacks that start after it is fully paid and live.
Listings disappear when a buyer cancels. Attacks made just before cancellation can still be claimed for a few minutes.
More questions? See the FAQ or the Selling Losses guide for the equivalent loss-selling flow.
